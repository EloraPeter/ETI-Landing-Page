Our Story

From Personal Journey to Global Institution

Florence Ofuokwu started ETI after experiencing how difficult it was to learn technology without guidance. Instead of accepting that challenge, she began teaching others online in 2021.

What started as a few students has grown into a thriving community of aspiring technologists across Africa. Her long-term vision is to build one of Africa's leading technology companies—developing software, educating talent, supporting innovation, and creating opportunities for generations to come.

Today, ETI is a community of learners, mentors, and innovators dedicated to building real-world digital skills. Our programs are designed to be practical, rigorous, and deeply connected to the needs of the technology industry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions people most often ask about learning with Elora Tech Institute.

Who can learn web development at ETI?

ETI's programs are open to anyone in Nigeria — and internationally via our online tracks — regardless of background. Our Web Development with AI and Python for Beginners programs are designed for complete beginners, while our Software Engineering and Data Science tracks suit learners who want a deeper, project-based dive.

Is ETI suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Several programs, including Python for Beginners and Web Development with AI, assume no prior coding experience and start from fundamentals before moving into real, hands-on projects.

How long are ETI's programs?

Program length depends on the track. For example, the Web Development with AI program runs 7 weeks, the core Web Development (Full-stack) program runs 10 weeks, and Mobile Development runs 14 weeks. Each program page lists its exact duration and format.

How much does an ETI program cost?

Pricing varies by program. As an example, the September 2026 Web Development with AI cohort is ₦250,000. Reach out via our contact page or Telegram community for current pricing on other programs.

Does ETI teach React and Next.js?

Yes. React, Next.js, APIs, databases, deployment, and SEO are core to our Software Engineering / Web Development track.

Are ETI programs online or in person?

Most programs run in a hybrid format, combining live online classes with practical, hands-on project work. Some tracks are fully online. Each program page notes its specific format.

Do students build real projects?

Yes. Every ETI program is project-based — students build and ship real applications during the cohort, not just complete exercises. Since 2021, ETI students have built 30+ projects across 5 cohorts.

Does ETI provide certificates?

ETI issues certificates on program completion. A dedicated certificate verification tool is part of our upcoming learning platform.

Where is Elora Tech Institute located?

ETI is based in Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria, and was founded by Florence "Elora" Ofuokwu. Our programs serve students across Nigeria and, through online tracks, learners across Africa.

How do I apply to ETI?

Applications for current cohorts are open on our Programs page. You can also join our Telegram community to get notified when new cohorts open.
